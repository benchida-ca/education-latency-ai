# Hand validation: 8 universities

Eight universities carry most of the weight in Finding 5 ("federal data can't see much of the adaptation"). Each was checked by hand on Oct 2, 2026, against three tests:

1. **It is a credit-bearing credential:** a degree, a concentration or track inside a degree, or a for-credit certificate, not a noncredit course, club, or news item.
2. **The year is plausible:** the archived page describes the program at that date.
3. **Federal data doesn't show it under cybersecurity:** College Navigator (nces.ed.gov/collegenavigator) does not list "Computer and Information Systems Security" for the school.
