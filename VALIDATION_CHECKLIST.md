# Hand-validation checklist: 8 universities (about 30 minutes)

**Why:** these eight carry the "federal data misses university adoption" finding. Each check takes about 3-4 minutes. For each one, confirm three things:

1. **It's a credit-bearing credential.** A degree, a concentration or track inside a degree, or a for-credit certificate. Not a noncredit course, a club, or a news item.
2. **The year is plausible.** Open the archived page and confirm it describes the program at that date.
3. **Federal data doesn't show it under cybersecurity.** Optional but quick: look the school up in College Navigator (nces.ed.gov/collegenavigator), open "Programs/Majors," and see whether "Computer and Information Systems Security" appears.

Write OK / WRONG / UNSURE in the Result column. If a row is WRONG, tell me and I'll rerun the numbers.
